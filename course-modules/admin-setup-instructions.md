## Frequently asked questions

**Q: How long does the actual setup take?**
A: 60–90 minutes for Tier 2 (Guided Deploy). Tier 3 (Done-For-You) is often spread over 2-3 sessions because there's more customization.

**Q: Do I need to be technical?**
A: No. Tier 2 and Tier 3 are explicitly designed for non-technical operators. The admin handles the technical parts. You handle the business decisions ("yes, automate that," "no, don't touch that yet").

**Q: What if I miss my scheduled call?**
A: No-show after 10 minutes = call canceled, you reschedule via the same Calendly link. No penalty for the first miss. Repeated no-shows may delay your support timeline.

**Q: Can I bring a team member to the call?**
A: Yes. Recommended if you have someone in operations or VA who'll manage the workforce day-to-day. Multiple people on the call = single point of training, less back-and-forth later.

**Q: What if I want the admin to deploy it FOR me, not WITH me?**
A: That's Tier 3 (Done-For-You). You pay $14,997+, you give us your Railway access, we deploy fully and hand over the keys. Tier 2 is collaborative — Tier 3 is hands-off.

**Q: What if I get stuck after the call?**
A: That's what your support window is for. Post in the Skool VIP channel (Tier 2: 30 days, Tier 3: 90 days) and an admin will respond. Average response time: under 4 hours during business hours (Central Time).

**Q: Can I upgrade from Tier 2 to Tier 3 later?**
A: Yes. Pay the difference ($10K). We pick up where Guided Deploy left off and take over.

**Q: Will the admin save my API keys or have access to my data?**
A: During setup yes (we have to enter them with you). After setup, we delete them from our records. Your keys live ONLY in your Railway environment variables. We never store them server-side.

## Common reasons for delay (avoid these)

❌ **"I'll have it ready by the call"** — Then the call becomes a setup-the-prerequisites call instead of a deploy call. Have the items above READY before booking.

❌ **No GitHub username** — We can't add you to the repo without it.

❌ **No API key budget** — If you don't have $20+ on your Anthropic account, the agents can't actually run.

❌ **Vague goal** — "I want it to do AI stuff" is not a goal. "I want comment-to-DM working on Instagram" is.

❌ **Trying to self-deploy first, then asking for help** — Fine, but tell the admin what state your environment is in. We'd rather start fresh than debug a half-broken install.
