## Two access tracks

### Track 1 — VIP Membership (Self-Install)

- **Price:** $97/month or $500/year (save $664)
- **Sold through:** Skool subscription
- **Onboarding:** Manual — admin team shares access within 24 hours of subscribing
- **Best for:** Operators willing to follow guides and get hands-on
- **CTA:** "Subscribe through Skool →" → links to skool.com/aicheatcodes

### Track 2 — Custom Install (Done-For-You)

- **Price:** Tiered by agent count
  - 1 agent: $3,000
  - 2 agents: $5,000 ($2,500 each)
  - 5 agents: $10,000 ($2,000 each)
  - 10 agents: $18,000 ($1,800 each)
  - 10+: custom quote
- **Sold through:** Discovery call → close → manual invoice (wire or invoicing portal)
- **Onboarding:** We build, install, and launch the entire system on a live call
- **Best for:** Operators who want it done right without touching a terminal
- **CTA:** "Book a discovery call →" → DM @qf_xcerebro or email operator@xcerebro.ai
